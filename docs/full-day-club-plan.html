Every new module runs the same way

  1. Florence’s brief5 min, whole group, one screen
  2. Play in pairsabout 25 min
  3. Catch her mistakecheck with evidence, not the “% sure”
  4. Fix and retrya clearer instruction, better examples or a fairer count
  5. Talk it over10 min, off screen, one question
  6. Passport stampone per module

What ties the day together

Your townBuilt in module 1 and kept all day. Each module ends by changing it.
FlorenceThe same guide everywhere, with a new kind of mistake in each module.
Club passportSix stamps, plus stars and medals, add up to the planner rank.
Leader’s page and club wallOne place to start each module, see who is where, and show every town at the end.

Build order. First the island map that opens the six places, with the shared passport and leader’s page. Then modules 2 to 6 one at a time, each play-tested before the next is started. All six run from one site with one passcode and share Eco Town’s look, voices and Florence. Still to decide: the club’s real hours, and whether these five topics are the right five.
