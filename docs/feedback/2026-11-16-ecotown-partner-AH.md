# Additional feedback: Alan Harrison (ComputerXplorers)

Source: WhatsApp messages, received 16 November 2026. Product and teaching perspective, not a separate playtest. Martin's observations (`2026-11-16-ecotown-parent-and-child-age8.txt`) remain unchanged.

## AH01 | The first building experience should teach prompting Florence
Alan agrees with Martin's concerns. If Eco Town teaches children about AI, their first experience of building the town should be prompting Florence effectively. Letting children build independently and then returning to the AI Labs and challenges turns the AI element into a frustrating repeat.

New compared with Martin: an AI-first learning sequence as the primary experience. Proposed direction, not a confirmed decision.

Recommendation: prototype a guided first town in which children tell Florence what to build, inspect her suggestions, correct unclear instructions and see the results in the town. Decide where free building belongs (after the guided sequence, or as a clearly separate creative mode). Test whether this improves understanding without losing the joy of independent building.

Priority: High. Related: F06, F08, F09; D01.

## AH02 | Challenge repetition is independently reinforced
Alan likes challenges but worries they feel repetitive after six AI activities. Reinforces F10, F12, F15. Recommendation: each later challenge gets a different problem, constraint or decision. Priority: High.

## AH03 | Open question: is Tiny Town achievable?
"Is the Tiny town challenge possible?" No failed attempt was provided, so not a confirmed bug. Check: the objectives, eight-tile limit, buildings and capacity can all be met at once; record at least one winning build; test whether an eight-year-old can find the strategy without adult help. Priority: High before a partner demonstration. Status: unconfirmed.

## Follow-up questions
1. Should the opening require children to use Florence before they can build freely, or keep both modes with clearer guidance?
2. What skill should each of the six AI Labs teach, and how should later challenges avoid repeating them?
3. Can the team demonstrate a successful Tiny Town under the live rules, and show Alan how?

## Owner decisions so far (17 Nov 2026)
- Side-by-side towns: the child's own town and Florence's town are separate and shown together.
- Florence's advice and any short film come before a level's blocks unlock, not after building.
- Challenges hidden for now; the modules after Eco Town replace them.
- Order (answers question 1): Florence first, then yours. At each level the child prompts Florence to build in her town, checks and corrects her plan, then their own town beside it unlocks for that level to build their way.
