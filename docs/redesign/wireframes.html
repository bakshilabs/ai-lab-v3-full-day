How to edit the words. Every piece of text that you can change now has a dashed outline. Click a piece of text, type the new words, and press Enter to save them. Press Escape to leave the text as it was. To put the original words back, delete all of the words and press Enter. Your changes are saved for everyone who opens this page.

How to use the whiteboard. Scroll in any direction to move around. Press Add a note card to drop a note in the middle of your view, then drag it by its top edge to where it belongs. Every screen also has its own Note button and a Comment button. To ask Claude for a change, write a comment on a screen and send it to Claude.

Feedback and decisions

Each set of feedback has its own section below, with the newest at the top. Press a section’s title to open or close it. For each proposed change, press Yes, No or Other. If you press Other, write what you would like in the box. You can also add a note to a Yes or a No. Your answers are kept as a record.
