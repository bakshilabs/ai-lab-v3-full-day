# Eco Town, level 4, "Food": third outline, in the market square form

The owner asked for this level to compare a big farm far away, an indoor farm in the town and community gardens in the town, and then agreed that it should be shown as one real place and not as three stacked slices. This outline describes that form. The level is built as a playable trial in this folder. Every word on its screens is a placeholder for the owner to judge.

## The place, and why it does not look like the other levels

The level happens in the market square at the edge of Eco Town. Three market stalls stand in the front of the picture. On the left of the square are the community gardens, between the homes. On the right is the indoor farm, a building whose front is open, so that three floors of shelves under pink growing lights can be seen from the square. Straight ahead, through the town gate, one long road runs across the valley and up a far hillside to the big farm, with its striped fields, its barn and its silos small in the haze. The far farm is really far and the two town farms are really in the town, so the child sees the distance before reading a word. No other level is a town square, and no other level has the glow of the growing lights.

## The camera, the time of day and the weather

The camera stands in the square at the height of the family, a few steps from the stalls, and looks across the square and out through the gate. The level starts on a summer morning. During the test the year turns in front of the child: the leaves go orange, snow falls, and the square is white. The season is the weather of this level.

## Proposed words (placeholders)

The job is: "The town needs fresh fruit and vegetables all year. Ask Florence to suggest plans for growing them. Then test her plans, so that you can make a good decision."

The scene line is: "Eco Town has cold winters, and fresh food does not keep for long. How could the town grow it?"

The sentence is: "Plan how to grow fresh fruit and vegetables that reach the market quickly, all year round. Grow them" followed by the first gap, "and grow them" followed by the second gap. The first gap offers "far away, where there is lots of land", "at the edge of the town" and "inside the town". The second gap offers "outdoors in the soil", "under glass" and "indoors under lights". The goal is fixed in the sentence.

The child's two words change what Florence shows. While the child chooses, a yellow place and a small sample bed appear in the town where the words point. When Florence answers, the plan that is nearest to the child's words appears first, with a yellow sign that says so, and the other two plans appear after it for comparison.

The three plans are "Community gardens in the town", "A big farm far away" and "An indoor farm in the town". They are in this order because that is where they stand in the picture, from left to right.

The two checks are "Short journey" and "Food all year".

For the community gardens, the result for "Short journey" is "The food was picked this morning and walked to the market on a handcart.", with a tick. The result for "Food all year" is "The gardens were full in summer and autumn. In winter there was little to pick.", with a dash.

For the big farm far away, the result for "Short journey" is "The food was picked days ago, kept cold and driven a long way. The lorries caused pollution.", with a cross. The result for "Food all year" is "The big fields sent plenty of food in every season.", with a tick.

For the indoor farm, the result for "Short journey" is "The food was picked this morning and came across the square by cargo bike.", with a tick. The result for "Food all year" is "The indoor farm grew salad, tomatoes and strawberries in every season. Its lights use electricity.", with a tick. This is the plan that is good on every check.

The recap is "Point to the place that still grew fresh food in the snow.", and the answer is the indoor farm. Any answer goes on.

## The test button, and what the child sees

The test button is "Take the food to market". One press sends food from all three places at the same moment, once in summer and once in winter.

1. In summer the handcart and the cargo bike cross the square in a few seconds and set out open crates. The lorry is still a small shape on the far road, with grey pollution behind it. It arrives last, and people carry sealed cold boxes to the middle stall.
2. The leaves turn orange and fall, and then the snow comes. The garden beds go white. The indoor farm glows as before.
3. In winter the cargo bike brings full crates again. The handcart brings one small basket. The lorry arrives last again, with plenty of cold boxes.

The camera then looks down on the three stalls. Each stall has a summer side, marked with a sun, and a winter side, marked with a snowflake. Full crates, an almost empty winter side and piles of cold boxes give the answer at a glance. The result cards sit below, and each sentence comes before its mark.

## The play break: "Grow a crate of food"

The camera goes to the place the child chose. For the indoor farm it looks straight into the open front of the building.

1. The child picks three crops from five. This is the personal mark.
2. The child chooses a crop and clicks a yellow shelf to put a tray of seedlings there. There are nine shelves and six trays, and the tall plants only fit the tall shelves on the ground floor.
3. The child clicks the water tank. Water runs along the pipes past the white roots, and there is no soil.
4. The child clicks the light switch. Snow falls outside and the plants grow anyway.
5. The child clicks each ripe tray to pick it. The grower loads the cargo bike, and it crosses the square to the child's stall.

The gardens use the same steps in a shorter form, with beds, a rain barrel and a handcart, and the tall plants go in the back row so that they do not shade the others. The big farm is shorter again: the child sows six of eight huge fields, starts the harvester and packs the cold boxes into the lorry.

## The mark on the town

The town keeps the place the child made, with the child's crops where the child put them, and the child's stall at the market with the food on it. The other two places go back to being an ordinary corner of the square, an ordinary building and plain pasture.

## How long it should take

The whole level should take about ten minutes. The test takes about forty seconds, and the play break takes two to four minutes.

## The two biggest risks

1. The far farm has to read as a farm at that distance, along one clear road. It is checked in the first picture.
2. The square holds three places, three stalls and the family in one picture, and it could become cluttered. The middle of the square is kept empty for that reason.
