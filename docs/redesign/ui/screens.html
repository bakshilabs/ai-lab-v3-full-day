Eco Town interface: every screen of one level

These are mock-ups of the interface at 1280 by 720, laid over real pictures of Eco Town on the world map. The pictures show the world map as it is today, so the town already has its buildings in every picture, and the rain is only sketched. Every sentence that a child would read is copied from the approved storyboard words in wireframes-data.js. Most screens use the Land level, and a few use other levels to prove that longer text fits.

The styles of everything inside a frame come from tokens.css, and the reasons are in DESIGN.md.

The town opens

1. Welcome on the world

The whole world is in view and the pin shows which town is open this week. Florence waves and speaks the welcome. The only thing to press is the dark button at the right end of her line, which is where the main button always is.

1Eco Town
1Eco Town

Welcome to Eco Town. This is a small town with homes, farms and a river. We are going to plan it together so that it is a good place to live.

2. The five checks

The storyboard has the welcome and the five checks on one screen. I split them into two beats so that neither one hides the world. The camera starts to fly towards Eco Town while the five tiles are on the screen. Each check gets one picture, which is the same picture every time that check is mentioned later.

1Eco Town

Test the town in five ways

FloodThis check shows what happens when the river rises.
Promote walkingThis check shows whether people can easily walk to the places they need.
Clean airThis check shows if the town will add pollution to the atmosphere.
NatureThis check shows what happens to plants and animals.
Staying warmThis check shows whether homes stay warm on a cold day.
