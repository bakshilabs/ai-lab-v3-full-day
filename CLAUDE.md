# ai-lab-v3-full-day

A **full-day kids' camp (AI Lab)** of eight hours: Eco Town, then the "Eco Town of the future" modules. **Every part is one hour, each module ends with a discussion section between children and their teacher, and a break follows each module** (decided by the user, 15 Nov 2026). Read `docs/full-day-plan.md` first: it replaces the ten-minute first versions.

- **Part 1, Eco Town:** the existing lab, one hour. Its lesson is "AI can be confidently wrong; check it". That lesson stops with Eco Town.
- **Modules:** drone delivery, robot training, roads for self-driving cars, town film studio, power control room, each grown to one hour (they were built as ten-minute first versions). Florence is a capable partner there, not something to catch out.

Read first:

## Redesign being explored (owner, 18 Nov 2026; proposals only, nothing built into the game yet)
- The owner prefers the clear look of the start page island (`js/home/island.js`: big plain blocks, few colours, hard shadows, stepped roofs) and is exploring a complete redesign in it. Design the outline before coding.
- **One world of six towns**, one per hour, at the island's scale (squares stay the same size): proposal page `games/ecotown/game/tests/visual/worldmap.html`.
- **Six weeks, six hours of play in total** (owner, 18 Nov 2026): one town each week, one hour each, six levels per town. The single island is too small for this (about 500 squares, room for 25 to 30 buildings); the six-town world is the map to design for. Each choice a child makes should leave a visible mark on their town.
- **No free play.** Every level: the child prompts Florence with word buttons, Florence proposes different design or operating solutions, the child looks at their outcomes through lenses and chooses. Flow diagram: `docs/redesign/game-flow.html`.
- **Wireframe deck of all 36 levels:** `docs/redesign/wireframes.html`, with its words in `docs/redesign/wireframes-data.js`. The owner has no OpenAI credits (18 Nov 2026) and said "don't use open ai for this", so Claude wrote the sample words on the wireframes. The page has a whiteboard view with note cards (artifact database, collection `notes`; each card records the screen it sits on in `where`) and comments for Claude; it is published at `https://claude.ai/artifact/1qGRpynEhx4Fn8A1bxq4ty`. That permission covers these wireframes only; the rule below still holds for the game's real text until the owner says otherwise.
- **The owner edits the wireframe words on the page** ("Edit the words" button, 21 Nov 2026). Each change is a document in the artifact database, collection `words`, with `path` (for example `towns/0/levels/0/job`), `text` and `was`. Before changing `wireframes-data.js`, read that collection and keep the owner's wording; an edit there wins over the file.
- **Oliver's feedback on the Eco Town wireframes** (21 Nov 2026) is in `docs/feedback/2026-11-21-oliver-ecotown-wireframes.md`. The 45 changes proposed from it are in `docs/redesign/wireframes-proposals.js` and are shown in a panel beside the whiteboard. The owner answers each with Yes, No or Other; answers are in the artifact database, collection `decisions` (document id is the proposal id; fields `answer` and `note`). Apply only the ones answered yes, and follow the note for the ones answered other.
- **Feedback rounds (owner, 21 Nov 2026):** every new set of feedback becomes a new round at the top of `wireframes-proposals.js` (its own section in the panel, which opens and closes). Old rounds and their answers stay as a record: never delete a round, a decision, or reuse an item id. When a round's answers have been carried out, give the round `applied: '<date>'`.
- **Words for children (owner, 21 Nov 2026):** say "check", never "lens" (a corporate word). Name each check with something a child can picture: "Clean air" not carbon (it is the easy title for carbon emissions, so its results are about pollution added to the atmosphere, not about air that is clean to breathe), "Staying warm" not winter power, "Sharing" not fairness, "Promote walking" for how far people walk (owner, 21 Nov 2026; it replaced "Walking distance"). Say "plan", never "solution". The child "tests" plans and watches the test happen in the town; ratings sit underneath what the child sees. Do not write "the child watches" in what the town shows, and say "no wind", not "still". The data files still use `lenses` as a field name.
- **Word buttons must be a real decision (owner, 21 Nov 2026).** The goals of a job (for example warm, low energy, long lasting) are fixed in the sentence, never offered as a choice. Never offer three words that mean the same (Build, Design, Plan). In each gap the options must not all be equally desirable: a child should be able to reason about which one serves the goal best, as in the Land level (beside the river, far from the river, high up and far away). The screen must also give the context the child needs to judge the options (`scene`, for example "Eco Town has cold winters"), because the best option depends on it. All 36 levels in the wireframes follow these rules (21 Nov 2026).
- **The first screen of every level (owner, 21 Nov 2026)** asks the child to ask Florence to suggest plans, and then to test them so that the child can make a good decision. It never says "Ask Florence where it should go" or "how to do it", because Florence suggests and the child decides.
- **No "add to your sentence and ask again" step (owner, 21 Nov 2026).** It was too advanced for this age group and was removed from level 4 in every town. Every level is now: ask once, test once, choose. Going back is the child's own choice: "Ask again" on the choose screen, and "Try another plan" on the result screen. The "Try another plan" path is drawn in every level of the wireframes: the child picks a different plan, sees it in the town, and then keeps it or goes back to the first plan.
- **Every level has at least one plan that is Good on every check (owner, 21 Nov 2026),** and the sample child chooses it. This replaced the earlier design in which every plan gave something up.
- **The child never picks which checks to use (owner, 21 Nov 2026).** The screen that offered a list of checks to select was removed from levels 5 and 6 in every town. The game always gives the checks.
- **Jobs on the end-of-hour screen (owner, 21 Nov 2026)** use simple job names a child knows, each with a plain sentence saying what the person does, and must be jobs that really do the work of that town. Not "Data analyst" or "Environmental scientist" for planning a town.
- **The recap was multiple choice (owner, 21 Nov 2026; replaced later the same day by the tap on the town, see below).** The child taps one of three answers; `recap` holds the question, the right answer and two other answers. If the child taps another answer, Florence gives a hint and the child tries again. There is no score.
- **Round 1 (Oliver, 21 Nov 2026) is carried out in the wireframes.** Every level in all six towns has a hands-on step (`hands`), a "Try another plan" button on the result screen, and a recap screen (`recap`). Only Eco Town has its own wording for what the town shows during each test (`watch`); the other five towns use the general caption.
- **Rounds 2 and 3 (Astra, 21 Nov 2026) are carried out in all six towns** (Eco Town first, the other five on the owner's request the same day; their buttons are listed in round `2026-11-21-astra-3`). Each level has one button that the child presses to start the test (`test`), and it replaces the hands-on step after the choice: "Make it rain", "Open the shops", "Make the materials" (the electric sawmill causes no pollution; say "pollution", never "smoke", owner 21 Nov 2026), "Take the food to market", "Keep the lights on", "Ring the school bell". The owner's standard for a test button: one press, the child causes something to happen in the town, the answer shows at a glance, and it tests the goal of the level. The School level is about children walking instead of going by car, not flooding (rain belongs to the Land level) and not similar distances. In every town: the context line (`scene`) is a short invitation ("Heavy rain is coming. Where could we put the homes?"); each result is a sentence about what happened, with only a tick, dash or cross beside it; a second try keeps a small picture of the first result and Florence asks "What changed?"; the recap is a tap on the town (`point`), with a question about Florence in only some levels (`askF`), and it never blocks progress. This replaced the multiple-choice recap and the hands-on step (`recap`, `hands` are no longer used). **Optional extra challenges: not for now (owner, 21 Nov 2026).** The deck opens with a flow chart of the Eco Town game (`ui.flow`).
- **Trial level and Astra's feedback on it (owner, 23 Nov 2026).** A playable trial of the Homes level is in `docs/redesign/concepts/trial/` (built by the design agent `play-break-designer`, which the owner gave the decisions about its play experience; published at `https://claude.ai/artifact/H3zRPAwjaJTVZeMBpZaDWm`; its words are labelled placeholders). Astra's feedback on it and on the seven play breaks is in `docs/feedback/2026-11-23-astra-homes-trial-and-seven-play-breaks.md`. The owner's answers: Florence is still never wrong or incomplete; the check keeps the name "Clean air"; for simplicity timber stays "no pollution"; the play break is about building the structure and enclosure of the home (for example the timber frame first, then insulation and walls), not decoration; no prediction step, check-Florence step or closing reflection (too complex); "Ask again" and "Try another plan" stay as they are; mouse only (it is safer and still works on an iPad). Astra's other points on the trial were accepted (the child's request must change the result, warmth comes from insulation and sun and pollution from making the material, evidence before ticks and crosses, not colour alone, keep the child's work on a second try).
- **Homes trial, further owner decisions (23 Nov 2026).** The family's home is a two-floor dollhouse whose front wall opens. A brick home must look and be built like brick, not with a timber frame. **Window placement is too advanced for this age group**: plans must not differ by where the windows are, and the child does not decide where windows go. The owner asked why concrete was dropped (the design agent dropped it when it made two timber plans); which three plans exist is the owner's decision.

## Rules for this repository
- **Never deploy to the original sites.** `ecotown.deltaops.workers.dev` and `bakshilabs.github.io/ai-lab-v3` belong to the original repository and stay unchanged. This repository's own live site is **`https://ecotown-future.deltaops.workers.dev`** (owner, 18 Nov 2026): Worker `ecotown-future`, KV namespace `ecotown-future-voice`, its own Durable Object code store. Deploy with `cd games/ecotown && node build.mjs && NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1 NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS=/root/.ccr/ca-bundle.crt CF_ACCOUNT_ID=9c5ee116be85caa02b46ec934ed28bbf node worker/deploy.mjs`, built from `main`. `/admin` is behind the existing Cloudflare Access app "Eco Town admin" (the new domain was added to it).
- The AI Lab as it stands (the town and six missions) is taken to be one hour. Do not cut it to fit.
- The GitHub workflow only runs when started by hand, and GitHub Pages publishes only from `main`. **Release routine (user, 15 Nov 2026):** when fixed or new module versions are ready, commit on the working branch, push, open a pull request into `main`, merge it, then run `ecotown-pages.yml` on `main` and check the site.
- Research and the other lab concepts (`research/`, `games/PLAN.md`, `games/labs/`) stayed in the original repository. Some documents here still refer to them.
- **Player and leader text is written by OpenAI, not Claude (user, 17 Nov 2026).** Claude must not write or reword any sentence that children or leaders read or hear (the game, modules, films, start, leader and trailer pages). Claude builds the pipeline and checks; the words come from the OpenAI API (`api.openai.com`, key added by the network proxy; prompt in `tools/text/prompt.md`). The earlier rewrites in `docs/feedback/2026-11-17-ecotown-text-rewrites.csv` were also written by OpenAI and may be rewritten by it.
- **Plain English in full sentences, everywhere (owner, 18 Nov 2026).** The owner could not follow compressed writing: fragments, dropped words, bold labels with clipped notes, dense tables, chains joined by colons or dots. Write to the owner in complete, ordinary sentences. The same rule applies to every word written or spoken in the game (it is in `tools/text/prompt.md`) and to any diagram or proposal page.
- Plain British English for the user. No budget or coins for ages 8–12.
- Unit tests: `cd games/ecotown/game && node --test tests/*.test.mjs` (295 pass on 15 Nov 2026; `model.test.mjs` "predict is fast" is a timing test and can fail on a busy machine).
- **Module rules:** a module adds files under `games/ecotown/game/js/modules/<id>/`. It does not edit `game.js`, `main.js`, `game.css`, `content.js` or anything under `worker/`. Nothing a child types leaves the device; no names; Florence only assembles from built-in choices.
- **Access:** an organisation has groups; a group has a join code; each child becomes a learner with a made-up badge. Leaders have a leader code and a page at `/leader`. The words "passcode" and "batch" in older documents describe the system this replaced.
