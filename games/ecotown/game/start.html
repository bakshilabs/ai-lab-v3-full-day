    How the day works

    Play it, talk it over, take a break

    Every part follows the same simple rhythm, so you always know what comes next.

    1. 🎮

      About an hour

      Six missions in each part, one after another, at your own pace.

    2. 🧑‍🔧

      Real jobs

      Each part ends with jobs that use these skills, from drone pilot to grid engineer.

    3. 💬

      Talk it over

      Screens down. Your teacher leads a short talk with questions shown large on screen.

    4. 🍎

      Take a break

      A break after every part, then on to the next stop.

    8–12

    Younger versionSimpler words, shorter steps and lots of pictures.

    13–17

    Older versionReal numbers, harder trade-offs and deeper questions.
